About Our Team

Cougar Robotics is the official FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) robotics team of Cinco Ranch High School in Katy, Texas. We're a student-run club team made up of students who design, build, program, and compete with robots at regional and state competitions.

Beyond competition, our mission is to grow interest in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math) among high school students and give them hands-on experience with real engineering, programming, business, and teamwork skills — skills that translate directly into college and career success.

Robotics is expensive. Between competition registration fees, robot parts, tools, travel to events, and team equipment, our annual budget runs into the thousands of dollars — almost all of which is covered by generous sponsors in our own community.
